## Completed in this tranche

- Principal guarded GUI now exposes a reviewer-facing Promotion Review page with eight operator gates:
  - Evidence
  - Signature
  - Trust
  - Freshness
  - Idempotency
  - Reviewer A+B
  - Baseline
  - Ledger
- Reviewer form exposes identity, ephemeral salt, lane and Vietnamese note.
- Raw reviewer identity and salt are not persisted by the GUI. Salt is cleared immediately after each attempted reviewer action.
- Reviewer lanes are constrained to `TERMINAL_PTY`, `PROJECT_RESUME`, and `OPTIONAL_GPU`.
- `APPROVE` and `REJECT` GUI controls are enabled only when a verified real packet is present and the trusted live upstream baseline matches the frozen v25.75 authority baseline.
- When the trusted upstream baseline drifts, `APPROVE` and `REJECT` remain disabled while explicit `INVALIDATE` remains available.
- Publication still goes through `PromotionWorkbenchController.record_review_action`, which performs a fresh live-baseline request immediately before append-only ledger publication.
- If upstream changes between GUI observation and reviewer publication, requested `APPROVE`/`REJECT` is converted fail-closed to `INVALIDATE` and records `BASELINE_DRIFT_LIVE_RECHECK`.
- A deterministic pure reviewer-action policy module was added to specify and prove button authority independent of Tkinter.
- Policy requires verified evidence, valid evidence/manifest digests, signature, trust, freshness, idempotency, official live-source provenance and a non-busy state.
- Policy proof includes MATCH, DRIFT, untrusted source, missing evidence and busy-state cases.
- Windows CI now compiles and runs the reviewer-action policy proof in addition to provider, ledger, workbench, controller, E2E and GUI-entry proofs.
