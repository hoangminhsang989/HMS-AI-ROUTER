- Durable COMMIT resumes at VERIFY only; duplicate COMMIT is forbidden.
- Integrated Official Auth switch with transaction journal and rollback evidence.
- Covers OFFICIAL_AUTH_SWITCH, ROUTER_RESTART, CLIENT_RESTART, CONFIG_REPAIR and LEASE_REELECTION.
